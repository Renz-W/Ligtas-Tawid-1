About the system

Built around people

Crossing the road should never feel like a risk.

Ligtas Tawid is a sensor-based traffic light prototype designed to make pedestrian crossings more responsive. It monitors the number of people waiting and activates a red light for vehicles once the configured threshold is reached.

For exceptional situations, authorized administrators can bypass the automated flow and change system configurations through the control panel—keeping human judgment at the center of every decision.

  • Sensor-based counting
  • Adjustable threshold
  • Admin override

Core prototype hardware

  • 01
    Arduino Uno

    The microcontroller processes sensor readings and controls the traffic-light sequence.

  • 02
    IR sensors

    Two infrared sensors detect objects and monitor pedestrian presence at the crossing.

  • 03
    Traffic LEDs

    Red, yellow, and green LEDs communicate each road signal phase clearly.

  • 04
    Wiring system

    Jumper wires and resistors connect every input and output into one working prototype.
